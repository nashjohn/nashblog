One book that has made a difference in my life was The Cross and the Switchblade by David Wilkerson.  It was amazing to see how the holy spirit guided Wilkerson in his life.  From wondering if God wanted him to go to New York City then to the holy spirit leading him to found Times Square Church and Teen Challenge. One Teen Challenge still exist in 2025 in Meskigan Michigan. That's the part of the Cross and the switchblade I love.
That's how I began coming back to Christ. My life was a mess and I figured if Wilkerson can do it so can I. Find the holy spirit. I'm stubborn most of that has been by fire. I'm still being disciplined it seems.

Jamie in his book, Living Fearless is more about Identity so much as dealing with fear. He invites you to listen to god and to know who you are.  Most people think having a relationship with god like that is just for certain people. The lol you know, maybe those apostles that gave Paul a hard time at Corinthians. That's why when i teach people to pray I say just talk to him like a friend. Tell him when your mad or whatever he knows it anyway.

So for me Jamie's book was a nice squeeze.
