Putting your foot down

2025-07-26

I was going to write about repentance and pride this week. We live in a fallen world so how do we put our foot down as a Christian. Last week at church Galatians 5 came up. In Galatians we have our checklist. Fruit of the Spirit - Joy, peace, patience, kindness, faith, gentleness and strength. Like wise there is a list of the fruit of the flesh.

This is how we measure ourselves in the Kingdom of God. If our enemies are spiritual and not of the flesh like a Ephesians says. Putting your foot down starts with oneself. Galatians 5 is our call and standard.
